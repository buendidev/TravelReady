# Privacy-policy and terms requirements

**NON-PUBLISHABLE DRAFT OUTLINE — qualified counsel review required.**
This is not legal advice, a privacy notice, a contract, or a claim of compliance. Do not copy it into the app/store as approved legal text. Fill verified facts only after architecture, provider contracts and retention decisions are settled.

## Privacy-policy checklist

- [ ] Identify controller/legal entity and contact details; DPO/contact where applicable. Do not invent company identity.
- [ ] Inventory collected/provided/derived data, local-only versus transmitted data, SDK telemetry, permissions and sources.
- [ ] Explain each purpose and approved legal basis separately, including consent withdrawal and legitimate interests where applicable; identify required versus optional data and consequences.
- [ ] Describe sharing recipients/roles, processors, subprocessors and international transfers/safeguards from signed evidence; do not imply EU-only processing from hosting alone.
- [ ] State approved category-specific retention periods or meaningful determination criteria, deletion, legally justified blocking and backup treatment.
- [ ] Explain access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaint rights, request channel and identity verification; reference the competent authority, including AEPD where applicable.
- [ ] Disclose profiling/automated decisions if actually present; do not invent an absence without auditing SDKs/features.
- [ ] Explain location/weather, shared trips/chats/directory visibility, notifications, subscription processing and sensitive document risks where implemented.
- [ ] Address children/age requirements, consent and guardian handling after counsel/product decisions.
- [ ] Describe security at a non-sensitive level without guaranteeing absolute security or unimplemented encryption.
- [ ] Identify effective date, versioning, material-change notification and accessible in-app presentation; assess web cookies separately if a website is introduced.

## Terms checklist

- [ ] Identify contracting entity, service scope, eligibility and account responsibilities.
- [ ] Describe Free/Premium features and limits only when approved; pricing, currency/tax, subscription period, renewal, cancellation and store/provider roles must match actual purchasing flows.
- [ ] Address applicable consumer rights, withdrawal/refunds and mandatory protections; counsel reviews enforceability. Do not invent blanket no-refund language.
- [ ] Define acceptable use, moderation/reporting, suspension/termination, appeal/contact and effects on shared content/data.
- [ ] Define user content rights/licenses and shared-trip/invite behavior; invites are single-use and expire after 24h, not a guarantee of secure implementation.
- [ ] Explain service dependencies, weather limitations and travel-document reminders without shifting away non-excludable obligations or implying professional travel/legal advice.
- [ ] Review liability, warranties, governing law/disputes and complaint channels for the target markets; avoid unenforceable jurisdiction clauses.
- [ ] Cover changes, termination, data retrieval/deletion and relationship to the privacy policy. Terms acceptance is not blanket consent to all processing.

## Publication gates

1. Engineering confirms the actual data flows, rule enforcement and rights behavior against the [data design](data-retention-rights.md) and [security baseline](../production/architecture-security-baseline.md).
2. Service owner supplies controller facts, approved features/prices, contracts, regions, retention schedule and request/support channels.
3. Qualified counsel approves notices, terms, lawful bases, transfers and retention/blocking; record reviewer, version and date separately.
4. Product owner verifies translations, app/store disclosures and permission/consent UI match implementation; obtain explicit release approval.

Reference: [official GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/?locale=ES), especially transparency under Articles 12–14. This source is not a substitute for consumer-law and Spanish-law review.
